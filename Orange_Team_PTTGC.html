Portfolio decision

PTTGC is exposed to petrochemical margins, feedstock costs, rates and demand. Assessing driver moves together helps distinguish broad market pressure from shifts in the company’s operating environment.

Financial channels

  • Brent and JKM LNG. Higher feedstock and energy costs can compress spreads when output prices lag.
  • PTT, IVL, SET and S&P 500. Returns in related equities and indices illustrate broader risk appetite. The historical inputs are price levels.
  • Thai yields and FX. Rates can change discounting and interest costs. The effect of a weaker baht depends on net USD exposures and hedges.

Targets and limitations

The price page illustrates a same-date close anchored to the saved PTTGC close. The regime page uses the saved 50-observation moving average as a reference for the mock Bull probability.

Historical regime definition

Bull when PTTGC close ≥ SMA50. Bear otherwise. The source export labels its early warm-up rows Bear.

Responsible use

Scenario weights are illustrative. Review data timing, dividend treatment and the source notebook’s yield-imputation flags before evaluating any trading model. Separate future labels from inputs and use chronological holdouts for validation.
